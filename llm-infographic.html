THE SIX STATIONS

🔪
1. TOKENIZER(Chopping board)Chops text into small tokens (word pieces) the model can digest.
🛒
2. PRE-TRAINING(Stocking the pantry)Reads the internet to learn: predict the next token. Months, thousands of GPUs.
👨‍🍳
3. ATTENTION(Chef tasting every dish)Transformer decides which earlier words matter most for the next one.
Pick your station by cost, control & freshness!
🧑‍🍳
📝
4. FINE-TUNING(Family recipe)Retrains on your examples so it cooks in your style or domain.
🧐
5. RLHF / ALIGNMENT(Food critic)Human feedback teaches it to be helpful, honest and safe.
🍽️
6. INFERENCE(Plating, one token at a time)Picks the next token; temperature = how adventurous the chef is.
➜
